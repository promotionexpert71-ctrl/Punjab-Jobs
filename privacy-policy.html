Privacy Policy — Punjab Jobs

Last updated: August 2026

Punjab Jobs ("the app") helps users browse job listings across districts of Punjab. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

The app does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to browse jobs. The app does not collect your name, phone number, location, or contacts.

Locally stored data

The app stores the list of jobs on your device so it loads quickly next time you open it. This data is not linked to your identity.

Job data source

Job listings are extracted from publicly available job notification PDFs using AI (Anthropic's Claude) and synced from a backend feed. No personal data from your device is sent as part of this process.

Third-party services

The app uses Anthropic's Claude API to read and structure job notification PDFs, and Google Apps Script / Google Sheets as a backend to store and serve the job list. These services process the PDF content only, not any personal data about app users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
